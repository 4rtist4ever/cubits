# Cubits

An 8-bit Rubik's Cube for the browser: twist, solve and unlock victory shows.

**▶ Play:** https://4rtist4ever.github.io/cubits/

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Drag on the cube | Twist a row |
| Right drag / drag beside the cube | Spin the whole cube |
| Scroll | Zoom |
| `1` `2` `3` | Push the left, middle or right column up |
| `W` `S` `X` | Push the top, middle or bottom row right |
| `F` | Turn the front face clockwise |
| `Shift` + key | Turn the other way |
| `Space` | New game / Pause / Continue |
| `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `Z` | Undo |

Keys follow the current view, not fixed sides of the cube. On touch screens, drag on the cube to twist a row and drag beside it to spin the whole cube.

## Features

### 🎲 A cube with character
Every side carries its own pixel-art figure: a smiley, a mushroom, a dino, a ghost, a bomb and an alien. Each piece also has a thin frame in its side's color, so you always know where it belongs, even when it shows only part of a figure.

### 🎉 Victory shows
Solve the cube and the figure facing you throws its own celebration. Six sides, six different shows. Can you catch them all?

### 🏆 Hi-score & collection
Keeps your best time and shows which victory shows you have already seen. Everything is saved in your browser.

### ⌨️ Mouse, touch or keyboard
Drag to twist rows and spin the cube, or play one-handed with the keyboard. The keys always follow what you see. On phones, a compact layout puts the controls under your thumb.

### ⏸️ Built for real games
The clock starts as soon as the cube is scrambled. A move counter, a live count of finished sides, undo, pause and continue, and a reset that takes you right back to the start.

### 🎨 Three looks, two modes
Bold one-color silhouettes, colorful classic figures or plain colors for serious solving, in light or dark mode. Everything firmly in 8-bit land.

### 🤫 A little secret
Somewhere in the interface something is waiting to be found. Crack its riddle and the Solve button earns a crown.

## Run locally

No build step needed. Download the repo and open `index.html` in your browser.
